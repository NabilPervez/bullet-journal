Privacy Policy

Digital Bullet Journal is published by Nabil Pervez Consulting (“we”, “us”).

In short: your journal never leaves your device. We don’t have accounts, we don’t collect or store anything you write, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.

What you write

Everything you enter — entries, dates, schedules, repeat rules and settings — is saved only in your device’s own browser storage, inside the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, permanently deletes your journal from that device. The app’s Settings screen lets you export a copy as a file; that file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, sign-in or cloud sync.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data — we don’t collect any.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files beyond a file you pick yourself to import.

Services the app relies on

Like any app that loads from the web, the following providers receive the standard technical information that every internet request carries — such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the time of the request. They do not receive anything you write in your journal.

  • Netlify hosts the app and delivers its files. See Netlify’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces the app displays. See Google’s privacy policy.

On Android, the app opens its pages using Google Chrome. Chrome’s own data handling is governed by Google’s privacy policy and your Chrome settings, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
